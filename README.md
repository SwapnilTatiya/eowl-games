# eowl-games

Public pages for eowl's Android games, served by GitHub Pages at
https://swapniltatiya.github.io/eowl-games/

- `sortlab/privacy.html`: Sort Lab privacy policy (linked from the Play listing and the app's Settings).
- `sortlab/switches.json`: remote ad switch read by Sort Lab. Values can only make ads rarer than the
  built-in limits (every 3 completed levels, 120 s apart). Set `"ads_enabled": false` to pause all ads;
  apps pick it up within 6 hours.
- `sudoku/privacy.html`: Sudoku Daily privacy policy (linked from the Play listing and the app's Settings).
- `sudoku/switches.json`: remote ad switch read by Sudoku Daily, same rules and limits as Sort Lab's.
- `numbermerge/privacy.html`: Number Merge privacy policy (linked from the Play listing and the app's Settings).
- `numbermerge/switches.json`: remote ad switch read by Number Merge, same rules and limits as Sort Lab's.
- `numberslide/privacy.html`: Number Slide privacy policy (linked from the Play listing and the app's Settings).
- `numberslide/switches.json`: remote ad switch read by Number Slide, same rules and limits as Sort Lab's.
- `nonogram/privacy.html`: Nonogram Daily privacy policy (linked from the Play listing and the app's Settings).
- `nonogram/switches.json`: remote ad switch read by Nonogram Daily, same rules and limits as Sort Lab's.
